# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML, CSS and ES modules. No build step, no dependencies, so it can be served as-is from GitHub Pages. (Chosen from the brief: "a github.io page".)

## Users

Project Sekai fans, mostly teens and young adults, playing on a phone in short sessions (commute, couch, between rhythm-game runs) and sharing the result in Discord or social chats. They know the cast and the cards well; some are newer and need the rules taught by the interface itself.

## Product Purpose

A Wordle-style daily game for the Project Sekai community with two modes, six guesses each:

- **Character:** after each guess, tiles say whether the school matches, whether the answer's birthday is earlier or later, and whether the answer is taller, shorter or equal in height.
- **Card:** a random 3- or 4-star card is shown heavily blurred and sharpens with each wrong guess. A guess is a card plus the art picked for it (untrained or trained); it reports whether its attribute, its group (the unit; any Virtual Singer counts as Virtual Singer), its character and its art match.

Success is a quick, fair puzzle people finish, share and return to the next day.

## Positioning

The only guessing game built around Project Sekai's own structure: units, schools, attributes and card art. The hints are the game's real data, not generic trivia.

## Operating Context

- Daily puzzle changes at the player's local midnight; a random puzzle mode is unlimited and untracked.
- Everything runs in the browser. Progress and stats live in `localStorage` only.
- Game data is generated from the community Sekai-World master database (the source IchikaBot also uses). Card art is hot-linked at play time from a community mirror; none is stored in the repository.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Six guesses per puzzle in both games.
- Character hints: school, birthday (month and day only, since the game gives no year), height in cm.
- Card pool: 3-star and 4-star cards (990 at time of writing); birthday and lower rarities excluded. Cards with only a Japanese title are shown with it.
- Group means unit. Virtual Singers have no school and one shared group regardless of which unit's version of the card it is.
- Third-party art hosting is outside our control; the game degrades to initials and a retry state if images fail.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Sekaidle.
- Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with SEGA, Colorful Palette or Crypton Future Media; the footer says so.
- The six unit colours (Leo/need, MORE MORE JUMP!, Vivid BAD SQUAD, Wonderlands×Showtime, Nightcord at 25:00, Virtual Singer) are the only saturated colours in the interface besides feedback states.

## Evidence on Hand

- `data/characters.json` and `data/cards.json`, generated by `scripts/build-data.mjs` from the master database.
- No user research, analytics or testimonials exist. Do not invent usage numbers.

## Product Principles

1. **The artwork and the cast are the content; the interface recedes.** Chrome stays neutral so card art and character faces carry the colour.
2. **Every hint is legible without colour.** State is always icon plus word plus colour.
3. **Teach by playing.** The empty state and tile legend explain the rules; no mandatory tutorial and no modal on arrival.
4. **Fair and deterministic.** Everyone gets the same daily puzzle; nothing repeats until the pool is used up.
5. **Respect the fandom.** Credit sources, claim nothing official, never host or resell the art.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for text and UI boundaries, in both themes.
- Full keyboard play (type, Enter to select, Enter to guess), screen-reader announcements for every result, respects `prefers-reduced-motion` and the system colour scheme.
- Touch targets of 44px or more for primary controls.
- Names and card titles may be Japanese; `lang="ja"` is set where the title is untranslated.
